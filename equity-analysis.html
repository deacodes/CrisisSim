What Does an Equity Score of 0.82 Mean?

CrisisSim evaluates fairness using a normalized Gini parity metric on a 0.00 to 1.00 scale. A score of 1.00 represents complete parity where transit wait times and evacuation survival are equal across all income and mobility tiers. In the baseline policy (0.54), low-income canal basin residents faced a 3.4× higher stranded risk than hillside homeowners. Under the active optimized strategy (0.82), that disparity ratio has been compressed to 1.2×.

Key Operational Finding
Critical Disparity Identified
Neighborhood 5 has the longest average evacuation time because Bridge 3 is its only direct route to a safe shelter. With 58% transit-dependent residents and 29% elderly individuals, Neighborhood 5 suffers an average wait time of 5.9 hours. When Bridge 3 closes at Hour 4.2 due to river overtopping, unassisted egress drops to zero.
Recommended Corrective Action
Targeted Mitigation
Open Temporary Auxiliary Shelter inside Neighborhood 5 & Pre-Stage High-Water Shuttles Deploying 12 municipal shuttle buses prior to Hour 3 and converting the Canal Community Center into an auxiliary dry refuge reduces average evacuation time by 2.1 hours and elevates district survival to 89.4%.
