Synthetic Data & Calibration Architecture
Educational Methodology
Spatial Synthesis Riverton is a synthetic city model constructed using open-source topological primitives and calibrated urban parameters. Topography follows a 10-meter Digital Elevation Model (DEM) with a meandering river valley and engineered canal basins. Road networks are structured with graph theory algorithms to replicate common transit choke-points.
Sociodemographic Calibration Demographic variables (vehicle ownership, age, mobility impairment, English proficiency, and household income) are calibrated against national census percentiles to model realistic disparities in evacuation capability and vulnerability.
